Delete your WitBlitz account

You can delete your account at any time. There are two ways.

In the app

  1. Open WitBlitz and go to Settings (also under Profile).
  2. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion takes effect at once.

By email

If you cannot use the app, email kindlingprivacy@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask for it to be deleted. We delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, including your display name and email address.
  • Your game results, scores, streaks and badges.
  • Your group memberships. A group you own passes to the member who joined it first; a group with no other members is deleted.
  • Your duel records.

Anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example, how many players finished a puzzle) may be retained; they do not identify you. The Privacy Policy has the full picture, including how long backups keep a copy.
