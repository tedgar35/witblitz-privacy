WitBlitz Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Who we are: WitBlitz is made and operated by Kindling (Tyler Edgar), Massachusetts, United States.

Contact: kindlingprivacy@gmail.com

This policy explains what information WitBlitz collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the WitBlitz iPhone app and the web version at play.witblitz.app. One policy applies to both; where something applies to only one of them, this policy says so.

The short version

  • We collect the minimum needed to run a daily game with accounts and leaderboards: your email, the display name you choose, and your game results.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads.
  • Your display name and scores are visible to other players on leaderboards, unless you choose to appear as Anonymous or Hidden.
  • You must be 13 or older to use WitBlitz.
  • You can delete your account at any time, inside the app, and it takes effect at once.

What we collect

Account information. When you sign up we collect your email address and the display name you choose. If you sign in with Apple, we receive the email address Apple provides (which may be a private relay address); we do not keep the name Apple may share. Your password is never seen or stored by us; authentication is handled by our provider, Supabase, which stores only a secure hash.

Guest play. Where WitBlitz offers play without an account, a guest has no email address and no password. We keep a guest's game results under a random identifier so streaks and scores work. A guest does not appear on leaderboards and cannot join groups. If you later save the account, it becomes a normal account under this policy. A guest account can be deleted the same way as any other.

Game activity. When you play we record your results: which puzzles you played, your answers and how long each took, your scores, streaks, badges, group memberships, and daily duel records. This is the data that makes leaderboards, streaks, and groups possible.

Technical information. To keep the app working we collect crash and error reports through Sentry (your device model, operating system version, app version, the technical details of an error, and your account's random ID), and usage analytics through Mixpanel (which screens and features are used, tied to your account's random ID rather than your name or email). Like any online service, these providers receive your IP address when a report is sent and may use it to estimate an approximate location, such as your country and city. On the web, analytics run only if you accept them in the cookie banner.

Reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your own device by the app, from your own play history on that device. We do not send push notifications and we do not store a device token. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your device settings.

Purchases. WitBlitz Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives your purchase receipt from Apple and your account's random ID, so the app knows your subscription is active.

Ad attribution (iPhone app only). If you install WitBlitz after seeing one of our ads on the App Store, Apple can give the app a token that says the install came from an Apple Ads campaign. The token tells us about the campaign, not about you: it carries no name, email, device identifier or advertising identifier, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. We use it to learn whether our ads bring in players. RevenueCat receives the token on our behalf.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or photos. We do not use the advertising identifier, and we do not use advertising or tracking networks.

How we use it

Where the law asks us to state a legal basis (for example in the United Kingdom), we process your information to provide the service you signed up for, and in our legitimate interest in keeping the game working, secure, and fair.

  • To run the game: accounts, puzzles, scores, leaderboards, streaks, groups, duels, and subscriptions.
  • To keep it working: diagnosing crashes and errors, and understanding which features are used.
  • To keep it fair: detecting cheating or abuse, including scores obtained by exploiting bugs.
  • To contact you about your account when necessary (for example, a password reset). We do not send marketing email.
  • To learn whether our advertising works, from the attribution token described above.

What other players can see

Your display name and your scores appear on leaderboards that other WitBlitz players can see. You choose how you appear, in Settings under Privacy, Leaderboard visibility: Show my name; Anonymous (you are ranked, but your name shows as "Anonymous"); or Hidden (you do not appear on other players' leaderboards). If you join a group, its members can see your display name in the group's member list, and the group's leaderboards and duels follow your visibility choice. A group's name is visible to its members. Please choose a display name you are comfortable making public. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run WitBlitz, and only for that purpose: Supabase (database and authentication), Resend (sending account emails, such as password resets and email confirmations), Cloudflare and GitHub (web hosting), Sentry (error reporting), Mixpanel (analytics), RevenueCat (subscriptions and ad attribution), and Apple (app distribution, sign-in, payments, and ad attribution). Each is bound by its own privacy commitments. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety or integrity of WitBlitz and its players. We do not sell personal information.

Where it is stored

Our servers are in the United States. If you use WitBlitz from outside the US, your information is transferred to and stored there.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, results, streaks, badges, group memberships, reactions, notifications, and duel records are deleted at once. If you own a group, it passes to the member who joined it first; a group with no other members is deleted. Anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example, how many players finished a puzzle) may be retained. Analytics and error reports stay with Mixpanel and Sentry under your account's random ID, which after deletion no longer belongs to any account; Sentry deletes error reports after a limited period. Copies in our database backups are deleted on a rolling basis, within about 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Choose how you appear. In Settings, under Privacy, set Leaderboard visibility to Show my name, Anonymous, or Hidden.
  • Delete your account. In the app, open Settings and choose Delete Account (also under Profile). Deletion takes effect at once. If you cannot use the app, email kindlingprivacy@gmail.com and we will delete it for you within 7 days.
  • Access or correct your information. Your display name can be changed in the app. For anything else, email kindlingprivacy@gmail.com.
  • Analytics on the web. Choose "Essential only" in the cookie banner, or change your choice later in Profile, under Legal.
  • Reminders. Turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device settings.
  • Subscriptions. Manage or cancel WitBlitz Pro in your Apple account settings.

If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, Canada, Australia, Singapore, or another region with privacy laws that give you additional rights (such as access, portability, correction, deletion, or objection), you can exercise them by emailing kindlingprivacy@gmail.com. We will respond within the time the law allows.

United States residents

Some US states (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and Utah) give residents rights to know, access, correct, and delete personal information, and to opt out of its sale, sharing, or use for targeted advertising. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising and we do not use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. To exercise your other rights, email kindlingprivacy@gmail.com. We will not treat you differently for exercising them.

Cookies and similar technologies

The web version uses essential storage to keep you signed in and to remember your settings and in-progress puzzles. Analytics cookies from Mixpanel are set only if you accept them in the cookie banner. The iPhone app does not use cookies. Our services do not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals; the cookie banner is how you control analytics on WitBlitz.

Children

WitBlitz is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact kindlingprivacy@gmail.com.

Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls on our database, and authentication managed by our provider. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you if a breach affects your information as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as WitBlitz changes. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: kindlingprivacy@gmail.com.
